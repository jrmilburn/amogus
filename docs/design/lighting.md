# Vision and lighting — #13

Impeccable extends the existing renderer with a nearby pool of readable station light, solid corner occlusion and a soft radial edge. Warm floor glows remain within the visible area; they do not reveal rooms beyond walls. There are no flashes or new decorative animations. DOM task HUD, role reveal and modal controls remain readable above the dark veil.

`VisibilityField` casts 96 regular circle rays plus three rays per nearby rectangular wall corner, with angle wrapping and axis-safe slab intersections. Rays stop at map bounds, static walls or future closed-door rectangles. Invalid/in-wall origins fail closed. Stationary positions reuse the polygon and render texture; camera motion and resize update only mask placement/overlay dimensions.

`VisionLighting` renders a white radial sprite clipped by that polygon into a reusable 1024×1024 render texture. An inverse alpha sprite mask cuts that shape from a screen-sized cold-dark overlay. The fixed texture bounds allocation independently of map size. The overlay is in screen space and uses the same letterbox projection as the world. Scene destruction releases the mask, scratch geometry and owned textures without destroying shared station/character assets.

The own predicted position centres vision during starting/playing. Crew radius is 650px × crewVision; impostor radius is 650px × impostorVision, chosen from private knowledge. Unknown roles use crew settings. Lobby and standalone rehearsals/previews stay unrestricted. Generic renderer-owned entity containers use `renderable` visibility gating after character sync, preserving the character's independent `visible=false` vent rule. This also covers future body entities registered via `setEntity`; no bodies or ghost gameplay are created in #13.

Finishing review identified self-occlusion near north walls because the sight origin is at the feet. The local engineer alone is reparented above the veil during restricted vision, with identical world transform and a separate letterbox clip. Its body/name remain readable without opening a hole onto hidden map or remote entities. It returns to normal y-sorting in unrestricted views. Remote entities never receive this exception.

Integration APIs: `setVisionMultiplier(n)` (0..1, crew only), `setClosedVisionDoors(rectangles)` (replace additional occluders), and `isPointVisible(point)`. #15 must restore multiplier 1 and open-door geometry on repair/reset and keep collision consistent. Task Use/highlight also checks visibility. Server positions remain public to clients: this is the explicitly planned party-game trust boundary, not anti-cheat filtering.

Automated tests cover doorway sight versus corner hiding, closing/reopening an aperture, axis-aligned rays, the ±π seam, world bounds, zero range, invalid origins, stationary caching and role settings. A moving-view benchmark over The Hollow with ten entity queries measured about 0.031ms p50 / 0.086ms p95 CPU-only in this session. This excludes render-texture generation, mask/filter submission and GPU work; it does not establish the under-2ms lighting acceptance.

Runtime diagnostics expose `renderer.stats.lightingUpdateMs` (CPU update and render submission, not GPU completion) and `lightingMaskUpdates`. Browser/device checks remain pending because automation reports no browsers: actual mask compositing, smooth predicted movement, doorway/corner views, labels/bodies, radius changes, dimming/reset, desktop/mobile/DPR resize, resource cleanup and total lighting/GPU budget.
